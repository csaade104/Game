## Art Assets

### Phase 1 (Procedural — no external art yet)
All tiles, sprites, UI elements, and particle textures in Phase 1 are generated 
programmatically using Phaser's Graphics and Canvas APIs. No external art packs 
are used yet. They are placeholder assets designed to match the final aesthetic.
